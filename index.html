Jiyoung (Serendy) You

Developmental Psychopathology Adolescent Antisocial Behavior Family Adversity Developmental Trajectories Justice-Involved Youth

My research aims to move prevention of adolescent antisocial behavior upstream, before young people reach the legal system. I study how family adversity — disrupted caregiving, household conflict, socioeconomic disadvantage — produces divergent developmental pathways, and when those pathways separate. This question came from practice. As a forensic psychology practitioner in a police diversion program in Seoul, I have assessed 110+ justice-involved adolescents, and kept meeting young people whose histories looked alike and whose outcomes did not.

What I Do

Research Experience

Applied Developmental Assessment

Forensic Risk Assessment of Justice-Involved Youth

  • Police Diversion Program (2024-Present) — Assess justice-involved adolescents (N = 110+) referred through a voluntary police diversion program, using semi-structured interviews and PAI-A to evaluate developmental, family, and offense-related factors; reports inform Family Court adjudications and prosecutorial disposition decisions

Youth Antisocial Behavior

Cross-National Study of Youth Gangs

  • Understanding Youth Gangs in Modern Society (2026-Present) — Collaborator on a funded cross-national study (10+ countries) examining youth gang perceptions and victimization; designing the Korean arm, with data collection beginning 2027 (PIs: Dr. Shannon Reid & Dr. Sungil Han, UNC Charlotte)

Risk Assessment

Risk-Assessment Instrument Development

  • Stalking Risk-Assessment Framework (2023) — Co-developed and validated two RNR-based instruments (SRAT-I, SRAT-P) for Gwangju Metropolitan Police Agency; coded 288 case files across 18 agencies
  • Implicit Attitudes Toward Victims of Sexual Violence (2023) — Coordinated an experimental study (N=200) using the Implicit Association Test; contributed to manuscript development

Developmental Vulnerability

Forensic Evaluations

  • Adolescent Victim Statement Analysis (2024) — Statement Validity Analysis (SVA/CBCA) examining how developmental vulnerabilities shaped an adolescent victim's testimony; submitted to a district court
  • Testimony of an Intellectually Disabled Victim (2024) — Credibility assessment integrating cognitive-capacity evaluation with statement analysis; submitted to an appellate court
  • Coercive Control in a Homicide Case (2023) — Assessment of coercive-control dynamics and testimonial validity; submitted to an appellate court
  • Psychological Autopsy (2023) — Analysis of psychological, relational, and environmental antecedents in a suicide case

Computational Methods

Computational Data Analysis & NLP Research

  • Computational Analysis of Psychological Manipulation (2023) — Text-mining of 5,000+ Instagram posts using Python; Best Poster Award
  • NLP Corpus for Investigative Interviews (2022) — Built a CBCA-coded NLP training corpus (3,000+ statements) for AI-assisted analysis of investigative interviews

Policy Research

National Policy Research & Consultation

  • National Policy Study on Illegal Gambling (2024) — Conducted literature review and background research supporting expert deliberations; contributed to the final report submitted to the Presidential Commission on National Unity
